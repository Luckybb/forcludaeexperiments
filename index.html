What I keep noticing

Most apparel brands don't have a traffic problem.
They have a clarity problem.

I've worked with apparel brands for years, and the same few gaps show up again and again. None of them are fixed by a bigger ad budget.

01

The homepage talks about the brand, not the buyer

Visitors decide in seconds whether a brand is "for them". If the first screen doesn't say who it's for and why it's different, good product never gets a chance.

02

Product pages answer none of the real questions

How does it fit? How heavy is it? Will it shrink? What does it look like on a real person? Every unanswered question is a reason to leave.

03

Collections are built for you, not for Google

"Drop 03" means something to you. "Heavyweight oversized hoodies" means something to the people searching. Collection structure is free traffic most stores ignore.

04

Content with no direction

Posting often isn't a strategy. Content should show the product being worn, the people it's for, and the reason to buy now, consistently.

05

Ads launched before the store can convert

Ads amplify what's already there. If the offer, pages and creative aren't ready, ads just make the leak more expensive.
