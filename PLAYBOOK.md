# BNC Group Playbook (what gets replies)

This is our second brain. Every reply, booking and objection gets added here, so each new email starts from what has actually worked. The skills read this before writing anything.

## How we use it
- **70/30.** About 70% of emails use proven angles from this file. About 30% test a new angle, tagged `test:<angle>` in LEADS.md.
- **Learn from replies.** When a lead replies, note what they replied to: the nugget type, the subject, the follow up angle, and their stage.
- **Learn from silence.** After 2 follow ups with no reply, count that angle as a miss.
- **Judgment stays with Ben.** Pricing, who we take on, and the calls themselves.

## Patterns so far
- Replies come from founders who are **already selling** and have felt pain from a past agency or redesign (One Vision). Pain that's already felt converts.
- **Specific, verifiable problems** described in plain words get answers. Generic "I can help your marketing" does not.
- Inbound and booked leads with **no brand details** waste call slots (Adam, Raqtiv). Always ask for details first.
