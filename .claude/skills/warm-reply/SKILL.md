# Warm reply (speed to lead)

1. Check all of these:
   - Gmail: unread or new replies to info@bncproductionz.com threads in the last 3 days.
   - Calendly: new bookings.
   - Any reply Ben forwards.
2. Check the thread first. If the other session already answered, only log it.
3. Location check: if the brand is outside the US, Canada, UK or Europe, decline neutrally ("our calendar is fully booked for the next few weeks"). Never mention location. Cancel any booking with the same reason.
4. Direct booking or reach out without brand details: ask for
   - brand name
   - store or Instagram link
   - where the brand is now (idea, launched, selling, new collection, scaling)
   - monthly budget for growth

   Say the call is confirmed after we review the brand.
5. Warm reply format (CLAUDE.md):
   - emotion first
   - 2 to 3 problems only, no solutions
   - one line on why it hurts
   - tease that there's more to show
   - CTA: the free 20 minute audit call, weekdays 11 AM to 3:30 PM Pacific, plus bncapparel.site
6. Send directly, no draft for Ben. Run enrich-leads on the brand first if it hasn't been enriched, so the problems are real.
7. Log the full text in MESSAGES.md. Move the lead to Hot leads. Add what they replied to (subject, nugget, FU angle) to PLAYBOOK.md. Commit, push, sync-doc.
8. Ben decides pricing, takes the calls, and chooses whether to take a client. Never promise prices or deliverables.
