Print on demand is the fastest way to launch a clothing brand without buying inventory. The first big decision is usually Printful or Printify. Both connect to Shopify, both print and ship for you, and both can work well. They're built differently, though, and that difference matters for your brand.

## The core difference

**Printful** prints and ships most orders through its own fulfillment centers. **Printify** is a marketplace that connects you to a network of independent print providers, and you pick which provider prints each product.

That one difference drives most of the trade-offs below.

## Quality and consistency

With Printful, the same company controls printing across its facilities, which generally means more consistent results from order to order.

With Printify, quality depends on the print provider you choose. Some are excellent, others less so. You get more choice, but you need to test providers and pick carefully.

**Tip:** whichever you use, order samples of your best designs before launch. Look at print sharpness, colors on dark garments, fabric feel and sizing.

## Cost and margins

Printify often has lower base costs because providers compete on price, and it offers a paid plan with additional discounts. Printful's base costs tend to be higher, in exchange for that in-house control.

Lower base cost isn't automatically better. Calculate your real margin: retail price minus base cost, shipping, payment fees and any ad cost per sale. A cheaper provider with more reprints and returns can end up costing you more.

## Product range and branding

Both offer a wide range of apparel and accessories. Check whether the exact blanks you want (heavyweight tees, oversized fits, specific hoodie styles) are available, and what branding options exist, such as custom neck labels, pack-ins and branded packaging. Branding details are a big part of making a print on demand store feel like a real brand.

## So which should you choose?

- **Choose Printful** if consistency and a simpler setup matter most, and your pricing leaves room for higher base costs.
- **Choose Printify** if margin matters most and you're willing to test print providers to find the right quality.
- **Either way,** design for the print method, order samples, and don't use the same default mockup on every product.

## The mistakes that cost POD brands sales

- Default mockups that make the store look like a template
- Designs that print too small or too dark on real shirts
- Missing size charts and fabric details, which cause hesitation and returns
- Prices that cover costs but leave nothing for marketing
