When you start a clothing brand, one of the first big decisions is who designs it. Search for help and you'll find everything from $10 gigs on marketplaces to full service agencies. Both can be the right choice. The wrong choice is picking on price alone.

I've worked with apparel brands for years, and here is how I'd think about it.

## What you're actually buying

"Design" can mean very different things:

- **A single graphic:** one T-shirt design, delivered as a file
- **A brand identity:** logo, colors, typography, labels, hang tags and packaging that work together
- **A launch:** identity, collection, store, product pages and a plan to sell

A freelancer on a marketplace is usually great at the first. A studio or agency should handle the second and third.

## Marketplace freelancers (Fiverr, Upwork)

**Good for:** single graphics, quick edits, tight budgets, testing ideas.

**Watch out for:**
- Designs that look good on screen but aren't set up for print
- Generic or template-based work that makes the brand look like everyone else
- No brand thinking: each design is a one-off, so the collection doesn't feel connected
- You manage everything else yourself: identity, store, launch

If you hire on a marketplace, ask for apparel work that was actually printed and sold, and confirm you'll get print-ready vector files.

## Design studios and agencies

**Good for:** founders who want the brand, the collection and the store to work together, and who are launching or growing seriously.

**What you should expect:**
- Strategy first: who the brand is for and why it's different
- A consistent identity used across products, store and social media
- Designs prepared for your print method (screen print, DTG, print on demand)
- Help with the store, product pages and launch, not just files

**Watch out for:** agencies that only do fashion design (tech packs, sampling) but not marketing, or marketing agencies that don't understand apparel production. Ask to see apparel brands they've built end to end.

## A simple way to decide

| Your situation | Best fit |
|---|---|
| Testing one design idea | Freelancer |
| Launching a brand that should look established | Studio or agency |
| Creator merch launch with a real audience | Studio with launch experience |
| Brand exists but isn't selling | Studio that does design and marketing |

## Questions to ask anyone you hire

1. Can I see apparel brands you've built, not just graphics?
2. Will files be ready for my production method?
3. Will the identity work on labels, tags and packaging?
4. Do you help with the store and the launch?
5. Who owns the final designs?
