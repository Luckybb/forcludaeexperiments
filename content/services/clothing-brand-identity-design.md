Customers decide in seconds whether a clothing brand feels legit. Before they read a word, they see the logo, the colors, the tag and the packaging. If those pieces don't look like they belong together, the brand feels like a side project, even when the clothes are great.

## What we design

- **Logo suite:** primary logo, wordmark, monogram and small-size versions that work on a neck label
- **Color and typography** that work on garments, on your store and on social media
- **Brand guidelines** so anyone making content for you keeps it consistent
- **Labels, hang tags and packaging** that make unboxing feel like a real brand
- **Applications:** mockups on apparel, bags, store banners and social templates

## Identity built for apparel

An apparel identity has to work in places most logos never go: embroidered on a cap, printed small on a sleeve, woven into a label, and stamped on a mailer. We design for those uses from the start, so the brand still looks sharp at an inch wide.
