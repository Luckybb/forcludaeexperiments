Print on demand is one of the fastest ways to launch a clothing brand with no inventory. It is also where many brands quietly lose sales: generic mockups, wrong variants, blurry prints and prices that leave no margin.

## What we set up

- **Provider setup:** Printful or Printify connected to Shopify (or your platform)
- **Products and variants:** sizes, colors and options mapped correctly
- **Print files** prepared at the right size and placement for each product
- **Mockups** that look like a brand, not a template
- **Size charts and product details:** fabric, fit and care that answer buyer questions
- **Pricing structure** based on base cost, shipping and margin

## Real example: a creator launch with a fixed date

For **Dream in Defiance**, a YouTube creator with 300,000 subscribers, we designed the clothing line, synced the designs to Printful with the correct size variants and print placement, built the Shopify store, optimized 26 product photos, added a custom preorder system without a paid app, and supported launch day live. The store launched on schedule to a real audience with zero downtime.

## Common print on demand mistakes we fix

- Every product uses the same default mockup
- Designs print too small or too dark on real garments
- Variants and sizes don't match what the provider can print
- No size chart, so buyers hesitate or return items
- Prices that cover costs but leave no room for ads

## Who this is for

- Founders launching a first brand with print on demand
- Creators launching merch for their audience
- Existing POD stores that want to look and sell like a real brand
