Most clothing stores don't have a traffic problem. They have a clarity problem. Visitors land, can't tell who the brand is for, can't find the fit or fabric details, don't see proof that real people bought, and leave.

## What we design and fix

- **Homepage** that says who the brand is for and why it's different in the first screen
- **Product pages** with fit notes, fabric weight, multiple photos, size charts and reviews near the price
- **Collections** named for what people actually search, not internal drop names
- **Trust signals:** reviews, shipping and returns info, real photos
- **Mobile experience:** fast, thumb friendly, with a clear path to checkout
- **Email capture** so the traffic you already have doesn't disappear

## Why product pages matter most

Every question a buyer can't answer is a reason to leave. How does it fit? How heavy is it? Will it shrink? What does it look like on a real person? A product page that answers those questions does more for sales than almost any redesign.

## Real example

For Dream in Defiance, we built a full Shopify store for a creator launch: product listings, homepage, sizing charts and policy pages, color graded and optimized photos for fast loading, and a custom preorder system. It launched on schedule to an audience of 300,000 subscribers.
