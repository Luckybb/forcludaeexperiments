Good apparel design is not just good art. It has to read from across the room, look right on a real body, print cleanly, and give a specific group of people a reason to wear it. That is the brief behind every design we make.

## What you get

- **Original graphics** for T-shirts, hoodies, crewnecks, hats and accessories
- **Collection planning** so every piece in a drop fits one story and one audience
- **Print-ready files** for screen printing, DTG and print on demand, set up for both light and dark garments
- **Mockups** you can use on your store and social media from day one
- **Colorway options** so you can test what your audience actually buys

## Built for print on demand and real production

Many founders start with print on demand, and a design that looks great on screen can print muddy or tiny on a real shirt. We design with the print method in mind from the start: placement, print area, color count and contrast. If you sell through Printful or Printify, files are prepared to drop straight into your products.

## Design that fits a brand, not just a trend

A trend can sell one shirt. A brand sells the next drop too. Before we design, we look at who your buyer is, what they already wear and what makes your brand different. Then every graphic pushes the same identity. You can see this in projects like the Glitch Collection for Dream in Defiance, a creator brand with a 300,000 subscriber audience, and the streetwear graphic series for Stillski and Hamrah.

## Who this is for

- Founders launching a first collection or a new drop
- Creators and YouTubers launching merch for their audience
- Print on demand stores that want designs that look like a real brand
- Brands with good products whose graphics feel generic

1. **Free audit** of your brand, store and current designs
