Paid ads stop the moment you stop paying. Search traffic keeps coming. For clothing brands, the fastest SEO wins are usually hiding in places most founders never think about: collection names, product titles and page speed.

## What we optimize

- **Keyword research** for your products and buyers, focused on what people actually type
- **Collection pages:** names, intros and structure around real searches like "oversized graphic tees"
- **Product titles and descriptions** that help both Google and buyers
- **Meta titles and descriptions** that earn the click in search results
- **Internal linking** so Google understands which pages matter most
- **Technical SEO:** indexation, duplicate pages, sitemaps and structured data
- **Page speed and images,** compressed and sized for fast mobile loading

## The most common apparel SEO mistake

Collections named "Drop 03", "New" or "Shop All". Those names mean something to you and nothing to Google or your buyers. "Heavyweight oversized hoodies" or "vintage band graphic tees" match what people search, and those pages can rank.

## Who this is for

- Shopify clothing stores that rely only on Instagram or ads for traffic
- Brands that want to rank for their product categories
- Stores with slow pages or messy product structure
