Most clothing brands don't fail because of the product. They fail because the brand speaks to everyone, launches with no plan, and loses every visitor who doesn't buy on day one.

## What we plan with you

- **Positioning:** who the brand is for, what makes it different and why that matters to them
- **Target audience:** the specific people most likely to buy, and where they spend time
- **Core message** that runs across your homepage, product pages, socials and ads
- **Launch and drop plan:** teasers, waitlist, launch day and follow up
- **Email capture and flows:** welcome emails, abandoned cart and drop announcements
- **Content direction** for Instagram and TikTok

## Launch planning from real experience

For Dream in Defiance, a 300,000 subscriber audience was waiting on a fixed launch date, with no margin for error. Design, Printful sync, the Shopify store, photo optimization and a custom preorder system all had to come together as one launch, and it shipped on schedule.
